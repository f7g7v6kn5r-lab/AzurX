Building the Commercial and Technological Infrastructure for the Next Era of Space
 

Space is entering a new era. Governments are no longer just building and operating, they are buying, partnering, and shaping markets, while AI is transforming how space systems operate and how data becomes insight. The hard questions about how to make this work are still being answered.

LAUNCHPAD brings together senior leaders from space agencies and commercial space companies for an afternoon of candid conversation about the future of government-commercial space partnerships.

Featured sessions
 

Featuring a distinguished international speaker line-up including: NASA · NOAA · DLR · Norwegian Space Agency · JPL · VAST · Axiom Space · Balerion Space, we will have two moderated panels that will tackle the following topics:

1) From Buyer to Market-Maker: How Government Enables a Commercial Space Economy — creating the demand signals, identifying what common services should be delivered by the government vs. industry, and partnership structures that let commercial space scale.

2) AI Applications in Space: Building the Infrastructure for a Sustained Presence — from autonomous systems on the lunar surface to turning massive volumes of Earth and space data into actionable insight, hear from leading experts applying AI in space.
